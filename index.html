Services offered

Areas of care and educational services. Select any image to view it full screen; on a phone, swipe through each gallery.

Fracture fixation

Surgical stabilisation of broken bones with plates, screws, nails or external fixation when a fracture needs operative care.

Joint replacement

Assessment and operative care for worn or damaged joints, including hip and knee replacement where appropriate.

Spine related issues

Evaluation of neck and back pain, nerve-related symptoms and other spinal concerns, with guidance on non-surgical and surgical options.

Sports injuries

Care for ligament, tendon, cartilage and muscle injuries sustained in sport and everyday activity.

Paediatric orthopaedics

Assessment of bone and joint concerns in children, including fractures, growth-related conditions and gait concerns.

Hand-specific injuries

Treatment of fractures, tendon and nerve injuries and other problems affecting the hand and wrist.

Diabetic foot

Multidisciplinary care for diabetic foot ulcers, infection and deformity, with an emphasis on limb preservation.

Physiotherapy

Physiotherapy at home or at the clinic, arranged after assessment, with a focus on safe movement, strength and recovery.

CPR training

Practical cardiopulmonary resuscitation training for individuals, families, workplaces and community groups.
