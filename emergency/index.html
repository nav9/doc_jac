Emergency & Trauma Care

In a life-threatening or time-critical emergency:

Go to the nearest emergency department immediately. Do not wait for a website reply, WhatsApp response or appointment. In India, call 112 for emergency assistance. Ambulance availability and local numbers should be confirmed for the patient's area.

When to go straight to the emergency department

  • Open fracture or bone visible through a wound.
  • Severe injury, deformity or suspected dislocation.
  • Inability to bear weight after significant injury.
  • New loss of sensation, weakness, or a cold, pale or blue limb.
  • Severe or rapidly increasing pain, swelling or tightness, which may indicate compartment syndrome.
  • Uncontrolled bleeding, major trauma or any rapidly worsening condition.

This is a general warning list, not a diagnostic tool. When in doubt, seek emergency assessment.

On arrival at casualty

  1. Proceed to the emergency/casualty reception and explain the injury and when it occurred.
  2. Follow the triage team's instructions. Do not delay assessment to contact a particular doctor.
  3. Share relevant medication, allergy and medical-history information with the treating team.
  4. If the surgeon has confirmed an on-call arrangement, ask the duty medical officer to contact the relevant orthopaedic team.
