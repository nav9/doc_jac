About this website

This website provides general information about professional services, consultation locations and educational content for Dr. ABC.

Privacy notice

Do not use this website to submit medical reports, prescriptions, identity documents or other sensitive personal information. The site is static: it has no database, no patient record system and no appointment booking engine.

A production website should have a privacy notice describing the data collected, purposes, retention, recipients, security measures and contact for privacy requests.

Do not publish patient photographs, X-rays, testimonials or clinical details without the necessary professional, ethical, legal and consent review. Consent alone does not override professional advertising restrictions.

Medical disclaimer

Information on this website is for general educational purposes and is not a substitute for a clinical consultation, diagnosis or treatment plan. Individual treatment depends on clinical assessment. In an emergency, seek immediate care at the nearest emergency department or call 112 in India. Do not wait for a website or messaging response.

Professional information

Before launch, add verified registration information, qualifications, scope of practice, hospital affiliations, consultation fees, availability and a monitored contact process. Dr. ABC's profile on this site uses placeholders for all of these details.

Technical information

The site is generated as static HTML, CSS, JavaScript and images. All internal paths are relative, so the built site can be opened directly from disk without a server. Consultation locations are read from js/configurable_data.js. Embedded maps use ordinary Google Maps iframe URLs without an API key, and no location data is collected by this site.
